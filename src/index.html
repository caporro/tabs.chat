Your AI workspace, one link away.

                
Connect cloud or local models, add instructions and MCP tools, and organize them into agents. Share a ready-to-use configuration through a link—or let an AI agent create one for you.

                
Runs entirely in your browser, including as a single portable HTML file. No agents.chat account or application backend is required.

              


              

                
Your edition

                
Chat, MCP tools, notes, Markdown, highlighted code, images and text/code attachments. 

              


              

                
Your data

                
Settings, chats, notes and keys are saved in this browser. Saved keys are not encrypted. Messages and credentials go to the services you configure, including any enabled proxy. Password protection encrypts shared links, not browser storage.
